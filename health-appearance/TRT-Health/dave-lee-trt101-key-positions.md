# Dave Lee — TRT 101: Key Positions (distilled)

**Source:** `~/Documents/Personal Docs/Medical/trt/TRT-101-Dave-Lee.pdf` (Shane's coach; got him started on TRT)
**Distilled:** 2026-06-30 · quick-reference alongside the research docs

## Philosophy
- TRT = interventional endocrinology, **lifelong**, foundation not magic.
- **Symptoms AND labs together.** Lab ranges are *depressed* (population T fell ~10-31 nmol/L in 2016 → 6-26 in 2022) — "above range" ≠ supraphysiological.
- **Biggest dial-in lever is diet/lifestyle, not mg.** "If you're 80% there, it's kilograms of food, not a few mg."
- Free T is the dial-in target; SHBG is "the main red herring." Don't chase it.

## Haematocrit (his clearest statement — backs the deferral)
- Don't measure right after starting (transient rise).
- Phlebotomy for high Hct **"often causes more harm than good — depletes iron + B vitamins."**
- Usually dehydration + body fat; hydrate before draws.
- True ceiling ~0.53; **"little concern up to 0.55 and even beyond, individual-dependent."**
- **"I have NEVER had a client needing a therapeutic phlebotomy due to TRT."**
- ⚠️ *Caveat for Shane:* this assumes an **asymptomatic** patient — it does NOT address epistaxis, which is the wrinkle that could change the calculus. See [[e2-lowering-options-research]] / hct research doc.

## Estrogen / AIs
- **Avoid any practitioner who prescribes an AI to initiate TRT.** "Estrogen is paracrine, made on demand locally — targeting a serum number is a fool's errand."
- High-E2 symptoms = **inflammation / metabolic disease / >15% body fat**, not E2 itself.
- → Leaning out (recomp) is THE endorsed E2 lever. No AI. (Shane's E2 207 / ratio ~20:1 = fine; Dave blessed T47/E2~200 initially.)

## Dose adjustment (backs a monitored dose-increase trial)
- "Don't be afraid to request a trial of a **mild dose increase** with follow-up bloods."
- **Adjust ≤25mg at a time** (50mg only for big initial mis-dose).
- **Bloods every 8-12 weeks before further changes.** Enanthate/cyp take 6-8wk to stabilise.
- **Never adjust on how you feel that day.** Be strategic, follow protocol.

## Target free T
- Start ~600-1000 pmol/L; **majority of clients 900-1200**; under-25s best 1200-1500; never seen a dialed-in man need >1500. (**Shane 1034 = mid-range; a bump to ~1200-1300 is within framework.**)

## hCG
- **Not required**; benefit is individual ("coin toss who responds").
- Try within first year **IF clean symptom baseline first** (so you can tell if it helps/hurts).
- Dose: **50-150IU daily**, or EOD 250-300IU; fertility insurance 3×500IU/week, 3-4×/year.
- hCG "estrogenic" side effects **don't resolve with an AI** (intratesticular aromatisation).
- ⚠️ *For Shane:* adding hCG now would confound the BP/epistaxis/CPAP stabilisation — establish baseline first.

## Protocol basics
- **Test Cyp/Enanthate, ≥2×/week** (twice weekly minimum; MWF or daily for fast metabolisers). Weekly/fortnightly dosing = red flag.
- Reject: pellets, gels, patches, commercial creams, undecanoate (Reandron/Nebido), AIs prophylactically, cookie-cutter "T+hCG+AI" protocols.
- Compounded scrotal cream (20%) is viable but injectables preferred; SubQ only after dialed-in (carrier oil matters — sesame/grapeseed/miglyol work, cottonseed/castor/peanut don't).

## Notable FAQ stances
- **Finasteride/dutasteride: "do not touch in any form"** (PFS risk).
- No binge drinking (liver = TRT outcomes; also Shane's gout trigger). Small amounts fine.
- Testosterone is cardioprotective; heart disease is a low-T / poor-bolus problem.
- TSH optimal <1 or close (Shane 1.3 = good).
