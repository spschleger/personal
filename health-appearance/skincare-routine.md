# Skincare Routine

**Started:** 2026-06-20 | Goal: simple, evidence-based routine. Restarting tretinoin after a few years off, with a mining/coal-exposure-aware cleansing plan.

## Evidence hierarchy (the big rocks)
1. **SPF daily** — highest-ROI anti-aging step, full stop. Also skin-cancer protection (Australian + sun-exposed mining work). Non-negotiable.
2. **Tretinoin** — best-evidenced active (collagen, texture, tone, fine lines; helps any TRT-driven oiliness). The heavy lifter.
3. **Vitamin C (AM)** — the *one addition*, added in Phase 2. Fills the gap tretinoin doesn't (daytime antioxidant + collagen cofactor). Higher evidence than copper peptides.
4. **GHK-Cu — skipped.** Overlaps tretinoin's collagen lane; the ceramide moisturizer covers barrier support during the restart. Not needed.
5. **Injectable peptide — skipped.** Thin human evidence, no protocol, copper-toxicity + research-peptide sterility risk.

## The products
- **Tretinoin compound:** tretinoin **0.025%** + CoQ10 0.05% + niacinamide 5%. Sealed Jan 2026 → fresh. (0.025% = mild entry strength; the 5% niacinamide buffers irritation + means no need to add more niacinamide.)
- **Cleanser: DHC Deep Cleansing Oil** — emulsifying oil, single-step. Removes SPF/sebum daily *and* cuts heavy coal/grime post-shift, without stripping the barrier. ~$40/200ml ≈ <$1/clean. (Budget alt: Hada Labo / Kose Softymo.)
- **Moisturizer: CeraVe Moisturising Cream** — ceramide-based barrier repair (the lipids tretinoin strips). AM + PM.
- **SPF50+** — dedicated, broad-spectrum, applied generously (Ultra Violette / LRP Anthelios / Cancer Council). AM only.
- **Vitamin C** — buy in Phase 2 (stable L-AA 10–15%, or gentler derivative). Not yet.

## Active plan (restart bare → add Vit C once adapted)
- **Phase 1 — tretinoin restart:** just **tretinoin (PM) + CeraVe + SPF.** No extra AM active — the ceramide moisturizer handles barrier support. (GHK-Cu not needed.)
- **Phase 2 — once tretinoin-adapted:** add **Vitamin C (AM)**, ramped in (few mornings/week → build; gentler derivative if it stings).
- **Switch on tolerance, not calendar** — add Vit C only when genuinely retinoid-adapted (most nights, no real irritation). Never put Vit C on still-irritated skin.

## Daily routine
- **AM:** water rinse (oil cleanse not needed AM) → [Phase 2: Vitamin C] → CeraVe → **SPF**
- **PM:** DHC oil cleanse → tretinoin (per ramp) → CeraVe
- Keep AM Vit C and PM tretinoin **separated** (different times of day).

## Tretinoin restart ramp (years off → go slow)
- **Wks 1–2:** 2×/week → **Wks 3–4:** 3×/week → **Wks 5–6:** every other night → **Wks 7–10:** build toward most nights, as tolerated.
- **Frequency long-term:** 4–5 nights/week captures the large majority of the benefit — nightly is optional, not required.
- **Application:** pea-sized, PM, on **dry** skin (~15–20 min after cleansing). **Moisturizer sandwich** early. If raw/red, **drop back a step** — too-fast is how people quit.

## Work-block protocol (Mudgee, post-underground-shift)
The harsh site soap inflames face/neck (esp. with tretinoin); the body tolerates it fine.
- **Face + neck → DHC oil cleanse** (single step: massage in, add water to emulsify, rinse). Cuts the coal without stripping the barrier.
- **Body → site soap as usual** — just keep it off the face/neck.
- **Then:** CeraVe on face/neck + SPF if surface-side.
- **Tretinoin → Sydney nights (Mon–Thu)**, skip work nights — captures ~all benefit AND gives the barrier a recovery window on the environmentally-harshest days. (Nightly *is* viable now that face cleansing is solved, if preferred — just do the DHC cleanse those nights.)

## Non-negotiables
- **Daily SPF** — tretinoin photosensitizes; without SPF the sun outpaces the repair. Especially Australian UV + sun-exposed shifts.
- **Book a skin check** — the genuinely important health item behind the cosmetic one, given cumulative UV exposure.
