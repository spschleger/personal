# Beijing online consultation action guide

**Prepared:** 27 August 2026
**Purpose:** obtain real specialist opinions on whether Yujie should travel to Beijing for in-person assessment for possible adult tethered-cord/lipoma surgery.

## Recommendation

**Cui Zhiqiang is the strongest disease-specific first opinion on the public evidence, but not the only approach.** His official Yuquan profile says he has performed more than 1,000 tethered-cord operations and lists published work specifically on lipoma-type tethered cord. Xuanwu remains the stronger large neuro-spine institutional pathway, and Wu Hao and Chen Zan both officially treat tethered cord.

**Best strategy:** submit the same packet to Cui Zhiqiang, Wu Hao and Chen Zan concurrently through Haodf. If forced to choose only one first, choose Cui. This ranking reflects publicly documented case fit, not proven superior outcomes for Yujie.

## The outcome we need

Do not ask for a promise of surgery from uploaded scans. Ask each surgeon:

> Based on these records, should Yujie travel to your hospital for an in-person assessment for possible surgery? What tests should she complete first, and can your team arrange the appropriate outpatient appointment?

A useful response gives a clinical reason, says whether an in-person review is warranted, identifies missing tests and names the clinic or team.

## Step 2 — Prepare the clinical packet

Create one folder containing:

1. Complete current MRI DICOM study.
2. Current radiology report.
3. Earlier MRI DICOM and report, if available.
4. One-page Chinese symptom timeline.
5. Current medicines, allergies, previous operations and major conditions.
6. A short list of the questions in Step 5.

Do not send only selected screenshots. Keep all original DICOM folders and files unchanged.

### Symptom timeline headings

- First symptoms and approximate date.
- Pain: location, triggers and change over time.
- Weakness: side, activities affected and progression.
- Numbness/sensation: location and progression.
- Walking/balance changes.
- Bladder symptoms and dates.
- Bowel symptoms and dates.
- Sexual or saddle-sensation changes, if present.
- Skin opening/dermal-sinus symptoms: drainage, redness, swelling, pain or infection.
- Current functional limitations.

Use dates and examples. Avoid phrases such as "sometimes worse" without saying when, how often and what changed.

## Step 3 — Upload the imaging

1. Insert the MRI disc into a computer.
2. Copy the entire disc contents into one new folder.
3. Do not rename, delete or reorganise the DICOM files.
4. Compress the whole folder as a ZIP file.
5. Sign in to Haodf and use its DICOM upload workflow at https://dicom.haodf.com.
6. Confirm that the study appears under Yujie's account.
7. Also attach the radiology report as a clear PDF or image.
8. Keep the physical disc for the Beijing clinic.

If the upload fails, do not substitute a few screenshots and assume the surgeon has the whole study. Resolve the DICOM upload or ask the doctor's assistant for the accepted transfer method.

1. 从现有影像和症状看，是否符合有症状的成人脊髓拴系，以及脂肪瘤／皮毛窦相关病变？
2. 您是否建议患者近期到贵院面诊，评估是否需要手术？
3. 如建议面诊，应预约您本人还是其他专门门诊？您的团队能否协助安排加号或合适的门诊？
4. 来中国前还需要完成哪些检查？
5. 如果考虑手术，初步可能是哪一种术式：单纯松解、脂肪瘤减容、皮毛窦切除，还是其他方案？
6. 目前大概的住院排期、预交金、住院时间和需要在北京恢复的时间是多少？
7. 如果恢复顺利，一般何时可以乘坐长途国际航班？哪些情况会推迟乘机？

我们理解线上问诊不能代替面诊，也不能保证手术。现阶段最需要确认的是：是否值得专程到贵院面诊，以及出发前需要准备什么。

谢谢医生。

## Step 6 — Evaluate the replies

Create one row per surgeon and record:

- Does the surgeon address the actual MRI and symptoms?
- Does the surgeon recommend an in-person assessment?
- What diagnosis or uncertainty do they identify?
- What tests are required before travel?
- Which clinic and surgeon should be booked?
- Does the team offer appointment assistance?
- Is surgery considered plausible, unlikely or undecidable before examination?
- Is any timeframe or cost given, and is it clearly provisional?

### Strong reply

A strong reply explains the reasoning, identifies missing information, recommends or declines an in-person review, and gives a concrete clinic next step.

### Weak reply

A weak reply merely says "come to the clinic", gives a generic operation description, promises surgery from uploaded imaging, or gives cost and timing without addressing Yujie's anatomy and deficits.

## Step 7 — Make the travel decision

Travel for an in-person clinic only if at least one named surgeon or team says the case warrants assessment.

Before buying flights:

- obtain the clinic date and booking confirmation;
- ask what tests should be completed before departure;
- confirm what original imaging and identity documents to bring;
- plan the first trip as consultation and work-up, not guaranteed surgery;
- use changeable travel arrangements where possible.

Do not book a surgery-length stay or a fixed post-operative return flight based only on the online consultation.

## Step 8 — Run insurance in parallel

Ask Yujie's parents for:

- exact product name and issuing authority/insurer;
- enrolment and payment receipts;
- screenshot showing the active benefit date;
- policy wording and exclusions if commercial insurance.

After selecting the likely hospital, ask Dalian medical insurance / 12393 to confirm active coverage, cross-province filing, direct settlement, deductible, reimbursement percentage and expected self-pay items.

Do not rely on the phrase "higher-tier insurance with a one-month wait" without the product documents.
