# Beijing surgery — how you actually get in, and how she gets covered

**Written 27 Aug 2026.** Purpose: replace family hearsay with the actual process, from official Chinese sources, for a Dalian-hukou Chinese citizen living in Australia who needs adult tethered-cord / cauda equina lipoma surgery at a top Beijing 三甲. Every fact is tagged **[C]** confirmed from an official/primary source, **[R]** reported by a reputable secondary source, or **[?]** unverified — verify by phone before relying on it. Chinese phrases are quoted so they can be used on the phone. Strategic comparison vs Sydney stays in `china-option.md`; this file is the *mechanics*.

---

## 0. Bottom line

1. **She is eligible.** Chinese citizenship + intact Dalian 户口 + valid 身份证 is all that's needed. Being abroad is not a bar; family can enrol on her behalf. **[C]**
2. **The "higher-tier, ~1-month wait" insurance the parents started is almost certainly 灵活就业人员职工医保** (flexible-employment enrolment in *employee* medical insurance): ¥499.59/month, benefits from the month of first payment / month after re-enrolment, no health declaration, no pre-existing or congenital exclusion. This is the right product. **[R, strong — one thing to verify, §3.1]**
3. **Commercial insurance is a dead end** for this: every 百万医疗险 excludes 先天性疾病 *and* 既往症, and the condition is already diagnosed. **[C]** If the parents bought a commercial policy, it will not pay.
4. **She does not need Beijing insurance.** Dalian cover settles directly at the Beijing bedside via 异地就医备案 at Dalian's rates against Beijing's catalogue. **[C]**
5. **Getting into the hospital is a separate, insurance-independent process:** online consult (好大夫) → 加号 / 特需 clinic → 住院证 → bed queue (1–4 weeks reported) → admission. Nobody needs a referral letter to be *seen*; a Dalian 转诊单 only improves the reimbursement rate. **[C/R]**
6. **Money:** complex lipoma release with monitoring bills ~¥80–150k **[R]**. Self-pay = prepay ~¥100–150k deposit. With 职工医保 the out-of-pocket is plausibly ¥30–60k; with 居民医保 ¥40–70k **[estimate]**.
7. **Timeline that actually holds:** 职工医保 paid early Sept → covered from Oct → surgery Oct–Nov feasible. 居民医保 → cover Dec 2026 at best, Mar 2027 at worst. Nothing beats Sydney public if Kohan offers a date inside that window.

### 2.2 The three doors

**Door A — remote first (recommended, do this week, no insurance needed)**
好大夫在线 consult with the target team. 宣武: 吴浩 from ¥312, 王凯/王作伟 from ¥112, 陈赞 from ¥42 (菅凤增 himself is 未开通 online). 玉泉: from ¥30. 七中心 李萃萃: open. **[C]**
- Upload: 好大夫 **accepts DICOM** (via app QR/link or a zipped CD/USB through dicom.haodf.com) plus photos and the report **[C]** → the I-MED disc is exactly the right payload; the PNG frames + zh-CN report are the fallback.
- What it yields: a plan, a cost estimate, and typically a **free 加号** (added slot) for a face-to-face visit — 陈赞's page states this explicitly **[C]**. No formal 绿色通道 exists; the path is consult → 加号/特需 → 住院证 **[R]**.

**Door B — book the clinic directly**
114 公众号 for a 专家 or 特需 slot on the day the numbers drop. 特需 is the realistic way onto 菅凤增's or 崔志强's own list. Self-pay for the consult (¥100–1000); whether 参保地 reimburses 特需 is doubtful — assume not **[R]**.

**Door C — 国际医疗部 / VIP self-pay**
Fastest imaging and beds, but everything self-pay at multiples of the public price (协和: deposit ¥20k, rooms ¥800–1500/day). Only worth it if speed matters more than money and insurance isn't active. **[R]**

### 2.3 Admission mechanics **[R unless marked]**
1. Clinic doctor issues a **住院证**.
2. Register at 住院处 → 候床 (bed queue). 宣武 neurosurgery: **1–4 weeks** reported.
3. Phone/SMS notice → **must present within 24 h** or re-queue.
4. Pre-op workup on site (repeat MRI incl. whole-spine, urodynamics, electrophysiology as they see fit): 3–7 days; Beijing's 2025 预住院 pilot lets these run as outpatient and fold into the inpatient bill, **异地医保 included** **[C]**.
5. Deposit (住院押金): no 宣武 figure published; Beijing 三甲 surgical deposits reported ¥50–100k [?]. Plan for the full estimated bill if self-pay.
6. Surgery → 3–4 days discharge for simple release; longer after lipoma/dural work; CSF-leak risk 2–10%; no lifting/bending 6 weeks; **no long-haul flight for ≥1 month, 2–3 months if any leak** **[R]** → 2–3 months in China minimum, as `china-option.md` already assumes.

Nobody at these units requires a referral letter to see or admit her **[R]** — bring the disc and the report.

### How to enrol in 3.1 **[C]**
- Online: 微信公众号 "大连市医保局" → 服务大厅 → 我要办 → **灵活就业人员参保登记** (needs her own 实人认证 — face check, so she does this step herself from Australia with a Chinese number), or 小程序 "大连市自然人社保缴费" → 灵活就业医疗保险 → 新办参保.
- Offline by a parent: district 医保经办机构 with her 户口簿 + 身份证 copy **[R]**.
- Pay monthly via the 小程序 / 大连税务 APP / bank debit. **Keep paying through the whole treatment and follow-up** — a break >3 months resets to the 次月 rule.

### How the money flows at the Beijing hospital **[C]**
- Principle: **参保地政策 + 就医地目录** — Dalian's rates/deductible applied to Beijing's covered-items list.
- 备案: 职工 → register 异地备案 via the 大连市医保局 公众号 / 国家医保服务平台 APP before admission (宣武's own guide calls this "第一步"). 居民 临时外出 in Dalian is 免申即享 (automatic). Either way, national rule: 备案 done any time **before 出院结算** still gets direct settlement; if she's discharged self-pay, 手工报销 back in Dalian.
- 转诊单 from a Dalian 异地转诊定点医院 (大医附一/附二, 市中心医院 etc.), registered at that hospital's 医保科, valid 1 year, can be lodged "住院前或住院后5个工作日内" — recovers ~10 points. Worth getting; parents can start it at 大医附一 with the MRI report.
- Self-pay items to expect regardless: imported dural repair / anti-adhesion materials, part of IONM (乙类), 特需 fees.
