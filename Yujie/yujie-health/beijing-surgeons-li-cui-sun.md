**Researched:** 28 Aug 2026 (three independent web sweeps, Chinese sources first; every claim below carries a source number). Patient lands Beijing **9 Sep 2026**.
**Case:** adult tethered cord — conus L4 tethered to 121 mm cauda equina lipoma, enhancing dermal sinus S1, left L5 root encased / S1 ganglion compressed, neurogenic bladder (300 ml residuals), progressive left-leg deficit + neuropathic pain.

> **Verdict.** All three are real, licensed, currently practising Beijing neurosurgeons — but they are at **three different hospitals**, not one team, and they are not equal for this anatomy.
> **1. 李萃萃 Li Cuicui** (PLA General Hospital 7th Medical Center) — the closest match by a wide margin: tethered cord is the core of her practice, she is Xiu Bo's direct trainee, publishes on complex/lipoma tethered cord, and also does sacral neuromodulation for the neurogenic bladder that may persist after release. **Book first.**
> **2. 崔志强 Cui Zhiqiang** (Tsinghua Yuquan) — high volume, tethered cord is his largest patient group, bookable Thu 10 Sep; but a mixed degenerative-spine practice, modest reviews, no tethered-cord reviews since 2023. **Strong second opinion.**
> **3. 孙振兴 Sun Zhenxing** (Beijing Tsinghua Changgung) — well-trained, Xiu Bo-adjacent, but tethered cord is now peripheral to his work. **Third opinion / tie-breaker only.**

## How to use this on the ground

1. **Fri 11 Sep — Li Cuicui**, 7th Medical Center (东四十条, central). Register via the hospital WeChat 公众号 or 114 *now*; her Friday clinics show only three September dates.
2. **Thu 10 Sep — Cui Zhiqiang**, Yuquan (石景山, west). Book the 特需 slot via Haodf/114 now; it is the day after landing, so treat it as opinion #2 even though it comes first chronologically.
3. **Sun Zhenxing** only if the first two disagree, or if 王贵怀's team at Changgung is wanted as an independent institution.
4. Carry the original I-MED disc, the 73 MB DICOM zip on USB, both report PDFs (EN + zh-CN), the Oct 2025 urodynamics report, and the one-page Chinese symptom timeline.

### Ask every surgeon the same seven questions (from the action guide), plus these three, specific to this anatomy
- Given a 121 mm lipoma with an encased L5 root and an S1 dermal sinus, do you aim for **complete or partial lipoma resection**, and what does your published series show for retethering in each?
- Do you use **intraoperative neurophysiological monitoring** (SSEP/MEP/EMG, bulbocavernosus) routinely for adult lipoma untethering?
- If bladder function does not recover after release, what is your plan — and for Li Cuicui specifically, is **sacral neuromodulation** something her unit would stage in the same admission?

### What is verified vs inferred
- Verified: identities, hospitals, titles, clinic days, Haodf statistics, the 2024 Front Surg paper, Xiu Bo's death and obituary, the 2012 co-authorship, civilian booking channels at all three hospitals.
- Self-reported / unaudited: all case-volume figures (">1,000", "~300/yr", "3000+").
- Inferred: Li Cuicui as the "senior remaining" operator at the 7th MC; Cui's clinic naming as a signal of practice mix.
- Not found: any surgeon's personal retethering rate, bed wait, or cost for this operation. These come only from the consultation itself.
