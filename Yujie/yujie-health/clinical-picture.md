## Neuropathic symptoms
- **New (reported ~8 Jul 2026): electric shock-like sensation at the edge of the vulva** — her words: shock/electricity feeling at the vulval margin/vestibule
- Quality is classically neuropathic (positive sensory symptom), in pudendal nerve territory (posterior labial/perineal branches, S2–S4)
- **Characterised (8 Jul 2026):**
  - **Left side only** — unilateral, consistent with a peripheral nerve problem (pudendal neuralgia is classically unilateral); argues against generalised vulvodynia and against central/sacral bilateral pathology
  - **Immediately adjacent to the vaginal opening** — vestibule/labial margin, perineal branch of pudendal nerve
  - **Spontaneous, not touch-provoked** — ectopic nerve firing pattern; argues against provoked vestibulodynia (where touch triggers pain)
  - **Relieved by direct pressure** — counter-stimulation/gate-control response, typical of neuropathic pain; notably the *opposite* of vestibulodynia, where pressure reproduces the pain (positive Q-tip test)
  - **Positional pattern: worst lying down; sitting and standing feel similar** — this is NOT the classic pudendal entrapment signature (which is worse sitting, relieved standing/lying). Doesn't exclude pudendal irritation from a hypertonic floor, but removes the confirming sign for Alcock's canal compression. Worse-at-rest/lying is common in neuropathic pain generally (reduced competing sensory input). Slightly raises weight on the sacral/spinal line (MRI already on the workup list).
