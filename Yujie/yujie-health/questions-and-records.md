## Questions to answer with her

### Bowel pattern — sensation
- Does she ever feel an urge to defecate before it happens, or does the BM just arrive without warning?
- After she goes, does she feel empty? Or does the sense of fullness persist?
- Does she have to strain to start? Or does it just come once it starts?
- Does her lower abdomen feel distended between BMs? Can she feel a stool mass when she presses on her lower left abdomen?

### Bowel pattern — stool characteristics
- What's the stool like in size? (Megarectum often produces very large-calibre stools.)
- Is the stool consistency uniform, or mixed (hard pellets followed by softer)?
- Any soiling, staining, or leakage between BMs?

### Episodic loose stools
- How often does she have "diarrhea days"?
- Is it loose stool with urgency, or more like leakage/staining without warning?
- Does it follow longer constipated stretches? (Pattern that follows a constipated stretch is classic for overflow.)

### Cyclical pattern
- Do any of her symptoms (bowel, bladder, sexual, pelvic pain) vary with her menstrual cycle?
- Dysmenorrhoea? Dyspareunia? Dyschezia (pain on defecation, especially cyclical)?
- Heavy or irregular periods?

### Childhood / history
- Any specific age she remembers the constipation starting?
- Was she ever assessed by a paediatrician for it? Any tests or imaging as a child?
- Any history of soiling / faecal accidents as a child?

### Medications
- Current and recent medications, especially:
  - Hormonal contraception (which one, how long)?
  - SSRIs or other antidepressants?
  - Antihistamines (chronic use)?
  - Iron supplements?
  - Anything else?

### Bladder
- Pattern of incontinence: with cough/laugh/exercise (stress) vs sudden urgent need (urge) vs both?
- Frequency of voiding during day and night?
- Does she feel her bladder is empty after voiding?
- Does she ever strain to urinate?

### Sexual function (when comfortable to discuss)
- Has it always been this way, or has it changed?
- Any pain with sex (entry vs deep)?
- Any sense of arousal that doesn't translate to orgasm?
- Any change with hormonal contraception starting/stopping?
