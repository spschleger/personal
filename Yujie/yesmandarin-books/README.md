# YesMandarin books

Xero-first reconstruction of Felicity's YesMandarin accounts from commencement.

## Definition of done

- Xero contains every issued invoice and credit correctly.
- Every relevant bank/card statement period from commencement is present.
- Every statement line is reconciled or explicitly unresolved.
- Invoice payments, other income, expenses, assets, drawings, contributions and transfers are correctly recorded.
- Opening/closing bank balances and Xero balances agree by period.
- Receipts and source evidence are attached or missing evidence is listed.
- GST registration history has been tested against actual current and projected turnover.
- Xero P&L, balance sheet, general ledger, receivables and reconciliation reports pass review.
- A tax working pack and short list of judgement questions are ready for Felicity or a registered tax agent.
- A small ongoing Xero process is documented.

## First discovery pack required

1. Felicity-authorised access to the YesMandarin Xero organisation.
2. Trading commencement date and ABN/entity details.
3. Every bank account/card used for the business.
4. Complete bank exports/statements from commencement.
5. Current GST registration status and any ATO correspondence.
6. Receipts, supplier invoices, contractor records and asset purchases available.
7. Any business transactions run through personal accounts.

## Principle

Use Xero's native features first. Write scripts only to remove demonstrated repetitive work or reduce error. Do not build a parallel bookkeeping product.
