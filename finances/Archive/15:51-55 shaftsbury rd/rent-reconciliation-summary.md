## The trap to watch

If the agent bills a full old-style week **24–30 Jun at $920** AND also starts the new **$960 week on 28 Jun**, you'd be **charged twice for 28–30 June** (~$394 overcharge).

This is not allowed — lease **clause 4.1**: the landlord can't require rent "for a payment period before the end of the previous payment period." Old and new periods must be **contiguous, not overlapping**.
