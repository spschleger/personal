# Work EA comparison — 2026-10-15

## Bottom line

The new agreement is **approved and live**, not merely proposed. The Fair Work Commission approved it in **[2026] FWCA 2762** and said it operates from **14 October 2026**; its nominal expiry is **7 October 2030** (new EA PDF pp. 1–2, decision [1]–[6]). Clause 3.1 also says commencement is the date in the approval notice (new EA PDF p. 7 / agreement p. 1).

For an **Experienced Miner**, the first new hourly rate is **$38.66**, up **$1.49/hour or 4.0086%** from the old EA's current/final **$37.17** rate. Because clause 9.2 says “First Pay Period after Commencement” and the pay week is Sunday–Saturday under clause 4.1, the first full pay period commencing after 14 October 2026 appears to start **Sunday 18 October 2026**. Payroll should confirm that interpretation and the first affected pay date.

Applicability still depends on the new, narrower coverage test: the worker must be employed by **Wilson Mining Services Pty Ltd** and engaged primarily on **strata consolidation works in NSW** (new cl. 2 and 3.4, PDF p. 7). Shane's offer confirms permanent employment by that exact company as an **NSW Experienced Miner**, described as **Level 2** under the old EA (offer PDF p. 1), but the offer does not itself prove that his current work is primarily strata consolidation. The new EA retains the **Experienced Miner** classification/rate title but does not use “Level 2”, so the practical mapping is unchanged by title, not expressly restated as a numbered level.

This is a source comparison and pay estimate, not legal or payroll advice.

## Exact rate comparison

### Experienced Miner hourly rates

Old clause 9.2's final/current column is **$37.17 from the first pay period after 1 July 2025** (old PDF p. 9 / agreement p. 5). New clause 9.2 provides (new PDF p. 12 / agreement p. 6):

| Stage | New cl. 9.2 rate | Increase over old current $37.17 | Percent over old current | Apparent first Sunday–Saturday pay period* |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Commencement | $38.66/hr | $1.49/hr | 4.0086% | 18 Oct 2026 |
| First anniversary | $40.01/hr | $2.84/hr | 7.6406% | 17 Oct 2027 |
| Second anniversary | $41.41/hr | $4.24/hr | 11.4070% | 15 Oct 2028 |
| Third anniversary | $42.86/hr | $5.69/hr | 15.3080% | 21 Oct 2029 |

Each anniversary step is approximately 3.5% over the preceding new rate. The table says the step begins in the first pay period commencing after the anniversary, and cl. 4.1 defines weeks Sunday–Saturday. `*` Dates are therefore calendar applications of those words, not a payroll confirmation.

Clause 9.2 says the listed wages are maintained for the life of the agreement. No grandfathering, red-circling or separate transition/absorption provision was found. Clause 9.1 continues to make the hourly rate inclusive of all Award allowances except shift loading, first aid and casual loading, expressly saying **no other site allowances are payable** (new PDF p. 11 / agreement p. 5; same old rule at old PDF p. 9 / agreement p. 5).

## Gross roster scenarios

These reproduce the documented assumptions only: 12-hour **day** shifts; ordinary roster Friday/Saturday/Sunday; Shane previously confirmed Thursday and Monday are additional full-overtime shifts for the five-day scenario. They are not a conclusion about an undocumented new roster.

For Friday–Sunday, base-equivalent hours are **64**: Friday `8×1 + 4×2 = 16`, Saturday `12×2 = 24`, Sunday `12×2 = 24`. For five shifts with Thursday and Monday each accepted as additional overtime shifts, base-equivalent hours are **112**: `64 + 12×2 + 12×2`.
