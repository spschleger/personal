## Headline

Treat the Wilson Mining job as a finite bridge: extract maximum income from it while running low-capex side businesses (Coal LSL, REA missed-call capture, consulting plays) that progressively replace it. Post-bridge, focus shifts to an enterprise-value play built on accumulated capital and freed time.

Capital strategy: liquidity and deployability over yield optimisation. Offset > ETFs > Super for cash parking, because the optionality to deploy into ventures is worth more than the marginal yield premium of less-liquid assets.

## Three phases

### Phase 1 — Bridge income extraction (current)
- Mining job = time-for-cash. Maximise hours where possible (OT, shift coverage).
- Side business runs alongside, funded by surplus.
- Capital parked in offset.

### Phase 2 — Income replacement
- Side business net ≈ mining net (~$140k+/yr).
- Step away from mining → reclaim time and location freedom.
- Stable assets continue moderate drip; excess to next venture layer.

### Phase 3 — Enterprise value play
- Time + accumulated capital + business credibility → build for exit/multiple.
- This is where real wealth is created. Not before.
