## Finding
Immigrant ancestor = **Carl → Francis Charles "Frank" Schleger**, b. c.1851, subject of the Kingdom of Hungary (Austria-Hungary), baker.
Naturalised Bundaberg 9 Sep 1886 (oath + Supreme Court register 8040: "Hungary", age 35, baker) [A].
Parents (1930 Qld death reg C3513/1930): Carl Schleger & Katharina Frank [A].
m. Mary O'Meara/O'Mara (c.1848 Ireland – 17 Mar 1932; parents Thade O'Mara & Johanna O'Sullivan), Maryborough June 1879 (reg C741/1879 as "Carl Schlegher") [A].
Arrived Maryborough 1878/79 per golden-wedding article (Scottish Hero; wife on High Flyer) [A article; no passenger index entry found].
Baker Maryborough/Yengarie/Gympie/Duncraggan/Mon Repos; land New Bundaberg 1887; AF629 Gooburrum 1889 (forfeited 1892); farmer Burnett Heads; d. 3 Dec 1930 Bundaberg aged 78; will 160/1931, executor son-in-law Albert James Marshall [A].
Press: "native of Austria" (obit 4 Dec 1930), "Mr Schleger from Austria" (24 Jul 1929). Family researcher (Cathryn King, Bundaberg; Burgenland Bunch) gives origin as **Eisenstadt/Kismarton** [B] — Hungarian until 1921, Austrian since → reconciles "Hungary" vs "Austria" [C].
