Publication: Morning Bulletin
Date Listed: 14/12/2019
Location: Rockhampton
	Late of Benevolent Living Rockhampton.
	Formerly of Nerimbera.
	Passed away peacefully on Saturday 23rd November 2019.
	Aged 90 years.
	Beloved Wife of Alphonsus Vincent ‘The Fonz' (dec'd). Loved Mother and Mother-in-law of Dean and Judy, Lindsay, Carmel and Noel, Veronica. Cherished Grandmother and Great-Grandmother of Michael, Shane, Rachel, Julian, Courtney, Kate, Ashlee, Tristan, Brendan, Hannah, Lachlan, Kobi, Oliver,Jaxon, Rumi, Madeline, Alistair, Audrey, Georgia, Isabelle and Declan (dec'd).
	A private Service was held in Rockhampton on Monday 9th December 2019 as per Rose's wish.
