C. JACKSON - the Rose Jackson problem
   Haystack size, stated honestly:
     - B2455 + "Jackson Robert"      -> 52 items (whole of Australia)
     - B2455 + "Jackson QLD"         -> 160 items (every WWI dossier with a Qld POB or POE and Jackson
                                        anywhere in the title)  -> of these only 15 touch the Wide Bay
                                        (Maryborough / Childers / Bundaberg / Gympie / Howard)
     - "Jackson Maryborough" all series -> 43 items
     - "Jackson Millie" all series      -> 2 items, both the same NSW man (Parkes NSW); NO Millie Jackson
                                           of Queensland anywhere in the Commonwealth record.
     - "Jackson Amelia" all series      -> 12 items, none Queensland.
     - "Jackson Rose" all series        -> 49 items, none Wide Bay.
   Four Wide Bay Jackson dossiers were judged worth opening and were downloaded in full (below).
