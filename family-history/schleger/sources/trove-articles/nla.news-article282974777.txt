More than 43,000 electors in the Wide Bay electorate will go to the polls today—Federal election day—to choose their representative in Canberra for the next three years.
Official number on the electoral roll is 43,597, but- some names have been deleted since the rolls closed. In the 1961 election, the number on the roll was 43,293.
I Four candidates are
seeking election—Mr. Brendan Hansen (the sitting A.L.P. member), Mr Walter White (Country Party), Mr G. Nichols (Social Credit Party) and Mr. D. Judge (D.L.P.).
In 1961, Mr. Hansen won the seat from the Country Party member, Mr. Harry Bandidt, by a majority of 2753. A Q.L.P. candidate and an Independent also stood.
In Dawson, Mr. G. Shaw (Country Party), Dr. D.
Everingham (A.L.P.) andMr. E. Eshmann (D.L.P.) are seekting election in place of the PostmasterGeneral. Mr. Davidson, who has retired from Parliament. 
Mr. Davidson held the seat in 1961 with a majority of 1960. A Q.L.P. candidate also contested the poll.
Total enrolment for Dawson is 39,751.
The three candidates seeking the support of the 39.860 electors in Capricornia are Mr G. Gray (sitting A.L.P.), Mr. M. South (Liberal) and Mr. A. Schleger (D.L.P.).
Mr. Gray won the seat from the Country Party Member, Mr. George Pearce, by 3732 votes in 1961. A Q.LJ?. candidate also stood for election.
The Civic Centre will be the main polling place in Bundaberg today for the first time in a Federal election.
A complete list of polling places in Bundaberg area, for both Wide Bay and Dawson, was published in the News-Mail last Saturday and on Wednesday.

Polling boots will be open for 12 hours from 8 am. to 8 p.m. and voting is compulsory, with a penalty up to £2 for not voting.
Voting will be under the preferential system, and a valid vote on both the House of Representatives and Senate ballot papers must have a . number in every square
against the name of candidates.
On each paper, a. voter should place the figure one in the square beside the name of the candidate of his first choice, the figure two in the square beside the name of his second preference and so on, until every square is filled.
Residents who will be outside the subdivision in which they are enrolled must cast an absentee vote, which can be done at any polling booth in any State.
