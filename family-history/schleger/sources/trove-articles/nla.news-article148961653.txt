25 Jun 1951 - BAPTISMS AT ST. STEPHEN'S
Maryborough Chronicle (Qld. : 1947 - 1954), Monday 25 June 1951, page 4
BAPTISMS AT ST. STEPHEN'S .
At the conclusion of morning service yesterday the following children were baptised by the minister, Rev. M. W. Roberts— Frances Leigh, the bonnie daughter of Mr. and Mrs. Don Harvey, of Lennox-street. Baby looked exquisite in her
cosy vayzella frock and cream woolies. In her party were her parents, her grandmother, Mrs A. L. Harvey, and grand-parents, Mr. and Mrs. W. J. O'Sullivan: grand aunt, Mrs. D. Grant, of Sydney; aunt, Miss Mirelle Harvey from Charters Towers, and her brother and sister. Graham and Carole who played an important part. During the afternoon the party was entertained to tea in honour of the happy oc-casion.  Darryl Craig, the infant son of Mr. and Mrs. G. T. Scott, of 195 Tooley-street, and his two sturdy young brothers were christened. Baby was wrapped in exquisite woollies, and the two wee men were cosy in knitted wear. Elsie Julie looked very sweet in cream georgette and woollies. With her was brother Denis and proud parents, Mr. and Mrs. A. C. Waye, of Richmond-street, and her godmother. Mrs. E. D. Johnstone, and Mr. T. C. Strehlau. Alan Kenneth, son of Mr. and Mrs. M. M. Taylor, of 27 King-street, wore a knitted tammy cap and matching jacket over his lovely frock with its drawn thread work and lace trim. He was accompanied by both his parents and his grand-ma, Mrs. Meredith, of Walker-street.. — "JANE."
