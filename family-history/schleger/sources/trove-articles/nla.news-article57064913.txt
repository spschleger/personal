Morning Bulletin (Rockhampton, Qld. : 1878 - 1965), Saturday 6 January 1951, page 3
ENGAGEMENTS
Mr. and Mrs. J. O'Hanlon, of 160 Murray Street, Rockhamp-ton, wish to announce the en-gagement of their fifth eldest daughter, Patricia, to Leo Pat-rick, second eldest son of Mr and Mrs H. C. Jeffcoat, Pros-pect Street, Mackay.
The engagement is an-nounced of Joan, youngest daughter of Mrs S. Boswood, 313 Bolsover Street, Rock-hampton, and the late Mr. T. C. H. Boswood, to Dallas Ed-ward, only son of Mr and Mrs A. E. Ewings, 119 Gladstone Road, Rockhampton.
The engagement is an-nounced of Yola Rose, young-est daughter of Mr and Mrs F. J. Johnstone, 140 Archer St, Rockhampton, to Alphonsus Vincent (Phonse) Schleger, Sydney, Fourth son of Mr and Mrs A. L. Schleger, Burnett Heads Road, Bundaberg.
