05 Oct 1940 - MRS. C STREHLAU
Maryborough Chronicle, Wide Bay and Burnett Advertiser (Qld. : 1860 - 1947), Saturday 5 October 1940, page 6
MRS. C STREHLAU
The death of Mrs. Clara Strehlau, an old resident of Thinoomba, oc-curred at Maryborough yesterday. The deceased was born in Germany. She arrived in Maryborough at the age of ten, and took up residence at Yen garie for a number of years, remov ing to Thinoomba 43 years ago. Her husband, Mr. Henry Carl Strehlau predeceased her by eight years. She
is survived by eight daughters, Eliza beth and Peterson (Thinoomba), Annie (Brisbane), Mesdames Sager and Johnstone (Maryborough), G Williams and Gerhamann (Bundaberg) and H. Joynsen (Howard), and two sons, Messrs. Frank and George Strehlau (Thinoomba). There are 29 grandchildren and four great-grand children. The funeral will move from the residence of her daughter, Mrs E. D. Johnstone, 22 Richmond-lane, at 11.30 o'clock this morning.
