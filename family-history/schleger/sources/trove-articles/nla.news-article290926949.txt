Alert (Maryborough, Qld. : 1899 - 1900; 1902 - 1937; 1939), Friday 26 April 1907, page 7
DOOLBI DOINGS.
This scribe has a 1000 tickets on Ken and Hannah for the Mat Stakes.
Juko and Emma as sweet as ever; wedding bells soon will ring..
Buck up you tennis players or your hon. sec. will be getting a bad rep.
Why don’t you get a lady of your own,
Schleger.
