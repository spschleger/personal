23 Feb 1940 - APPLE TREE CREEK PIONEER
Isis Recorder (Childers, Qld. : 1931 - 1965), Friday 23 February 1940, page 3
APPLE TREE CREEK PIONEER
Death of Mrs. H. A. Hughes
The death occurred on Wednesday of a well-known resident of Maryborough, Mrs. Harriet Ann Hughes, aged 80 years. Born- at St. Austell
(England), the deceased came out to Australia at the age of four years with her parents, the late Mr. and Mrs. Thomas Curtis, landing at Adelaide in 1863. Shortly afterwards the family came to Queensland, and Mr. Curtis became manager of the Tee-bar Mines in 1865. He moved to Maryborough and took charge of the ferry running across the river from Maryborough to Tinana. The late Mrs. Hughes was married twice. Her first husband, the late Mr. George Johnstone, was a road contractor end she and her husband were among the first settlers at Apple Tree Creek, Childers. Children by the late Mrs. Hughes' first marriage were Messrs. William John and George Vincent Johnstone (Maryborough), Frederick Jason ("Toby") Johnstone (Colosseum, Bundaberg) and Mesdames Marcus Boge (Maryborough), and T. M. Schmidt (Gym-pie). She was predeceased by her second husband and by two sons and a daughter. There are eleven grandchildren. — (''Chronicle".)
