MARYBOROUGH.
JUDGING DAY.
MARYBOROUGH, June 1.
The -16 th annual exhibition of the Wide Bay and Burnett Pastoral and Agricultural Society was commenced to-day. The society has been remarkably unfortunate for many years in regard to weather conditions, and those prevailing to-day were not altogether auspicious for the official opening to-morrow. Several showers fell, but to-night the prospects were brighter. To-day was devoted exclusively to judging, and consequently the attendance was not large. Although the show suffered as a result of the dry season, it ranked well up to the standard of previous years. One very noticeable feature in the pavilion was the increase in the industrial exhibits. Agriculture was not Very strongly represented, but the exhibits were of high quulity, particularly potatoes. One of the best collections of fruits made for years was on view, the citrus fruits being an outstanding feature. Samples of fruits and potatoes grown for export by growers of the Gooniboorian, Mount Eaton, and Correllq 4iStriiflt3 up a creditable display.
The Maryborough show has come to bo regarded as one of the best in provincial towns for dairy cattle, and although the section was weaker numerically than last year, the exhibits were splendid, the Illawarras and Milking Shorthorns being the strongest, class. The awards wero well distributed over various breeders, the championships going to Mr. W. G. Currant's Young Richmond and Damsel II. respectively. In the Jersey class the Nanango breeder, Mr. V. Goodger, was again very successful, his recently-imported bull Foxgloves King winning the championship, while that honour fell to the same owner’s cow. Golden Spray. Mr IT. M'D. Livingstone had a clear field in the Ayrshire class, and his cattle were very highly commented upon. The champion group wont to Mr. V. Goodgcr's Jerseys, with Mr. H. Livingstone's Ayrshires second. The ring events were restricted to judging the led classes. A strong feature of the exhibits was the draught stallions, while the ponies wero exceptionally good. The show will be officially opened to-morrow by Mr. John Macdonald (Chairman of the National Association).
