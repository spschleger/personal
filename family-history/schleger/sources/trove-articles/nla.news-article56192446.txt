22 Jan 1941 - STATE SCHOLARSHIP EXAMINATION Central District Successes
Morning Bulletin (Rockhampton, Qld. : 1878 - 1965), Wednesday 22 January 1941, page 8
STATE SCHOLARSHIP
EXAMINATION
Central District Successes
BRISBANE, January 21
Of 8645 candidates for State scholarships who sat for the examination 5299 passed, representing 61.3 per cent.
The Lilley gold medal for the boy and girl from State primary schools with the highest passes were won by Bernard Samuel Backstrom, with 90.8 per cent, and Irene Catherine Myles, with 89.6 per cent. Irene Myles also won the T. J. Ryan memorial medal.
Samuel Backstrom lives at Yeronga, South Brisbane, and Irene Myles at Yeppoon.
The following filled the first twelve places in the State: Bernard S. Backstrom, Irene C. Myles, Glen R. Whittle, Ralph L. Doherty, John P. Malos, George B. Langang, Russell G. Wilson, Ivan B. Berglin, Betty J. C. Lamont, Clive George Cook, Lyndall E. Hughes, Inez L. Martin.
Samuel Backstrom bettered the previous record for the scholarship by .2 per cent. His mathematics paper was perfect and
earned him full marks.
His father, who is a civil engineer in the
Tramway Department, gained third place in  
the State when he took his scholarship, sec-ond in the Junior and first in the Senior.
The scholarships entitle holders to at-tend any approved secondary school for two years. The following candidates in the Central
Division were successful:
