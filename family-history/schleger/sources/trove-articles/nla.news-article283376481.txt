09 Sep 1964 - OBITUARY
News-Mail (Bundaberg, Qld : 1961 - 1965), Wednesday 9 September 1964, page 3
OBITUARY
MR. A. L. SCHLEGER
Mr. ‘ Alphonso Leo Schleger, of Burnett Heads Road, died at the Mater Misericordiae. Hospital last night aged 78. He was born at Bundaberg, and . was the fifth child of Mr.
and Mrs. F. G. Schleger, pioneer farmers at Burnett Heads. He began farming in Burnett Heads Road when a young man, and still occupied the farm at the time of his death. He took a keen interest iri sport and welfare organisations at Burnett Heads and was. for many years secretary of the Burnett Heads School Committee,
Mr. Schleger was married in Brisbane to Miss 'Mary Ellen Napier, who predeceased him in 1951. He is survived by four sons, Francis (Sydney),
Kevin (Bundaberg), Ter-, rence ■ (Burnett Heads), Alphonsus (Rockhampton)- six daughters. Sister Mary Scholastica- (Barcaldine), Mrs. O. Hayes (Toowoomba), Mrs. E. Stitt (Barolin), Mrs; J. Moroney (Brisbane), Mrs. K. Derinien (Bundaberg), Miss Bernadine Schleger (Brisbane); a brother, Raphael (Brisbane); and three . sisters, Mrs. B. Chalmers (Bundaberg), Mrs. M. O’Leary (Bundaberg), and Mrs. C. Marshall (Brisbane).
The funeral will move from Holy Rosary Roman Catholic Church tomorrow, after prayers, at 2.30 p.m. for . the Catholic Cemetery. John Novakoski is the funeral director.
