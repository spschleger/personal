05 Aug 1915 - Suicide at Burnett Heads.
Brisbane Courier (Qld. : 1864 - 1933), Thursday 5 August 1915, page 8
Suicide at Burnett Heads.
Ignatius Schleger, aged 32 years, who resided with his parents at Burnett Heads, was found hanging in a barn this morning (our Bundaberg correspondent advised last night) . The body was
subsequently taken possession of by the police, and a post-mortem examination will be held tomorrow. The deceased had been in a weak state of health for some time.
