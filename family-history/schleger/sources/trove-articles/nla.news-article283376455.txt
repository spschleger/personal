09 Sep 1964 - Family Notices
News-Mail (Bundaberg, Qld : 1961 - 1965), Wednesday 9 September 1964, page 4
DEATH
SCHLEGER. — At the Mater Misercordiae Hospital, West Bundaberg on' September 8, 1964, Alphonso Leo, widower of Mary Ellen, and loved father of Francis Charles (Sydney); Valerie Mary (Sister Mary Scholastics, Barcaldine); Kevin (Bundaberg); Terrence (Burnett Heads); Olga (Mrs. O. Hayes, Toowoomba); Dalphine (Mrs. E. Stitt, Barolin); Alphonsus (Rockhampton); Maureen (Mrs. J. Moroney, Brisbane); Regis (Mrs. K Dennien, Bundabergl; Bernadine (Brisbane), and loved brother of Raphael (Brisbane); Mrs. B. Chalmers (Bundaberg); Mrs. M. O’Leary (Bundaberg); Mrs. C. Marshall (Brisbane), late of Burnett Heads Road. Aged 78 years. — Rest In Peace
FUNERAL
SCHLEGER. — The relatives and friends of the late Alphonso Leo Schleger, also the friends of his family, brother and sisters, are respectfully invited to attend his funeral which is appointed to move from the Holy Rosary Church, Barolin Street tomorrow (Thursday) after prayers commencing at 2.30 p.m.. for interment in the Catholic Cemetery. Requiem Mass at 6.30 a.m. tomorrow (Thursday).— JOHN NOVAKOSKI, . Funeral Director.
