26 Jun 1937 - Obituary.
Daily Mercury (Mackay, Qld. : 1906 - 1954), Saturday 26 June 1937, page 4
        Obituary.
        MR JOHN JARVIS HOLMES.
There passed away at Mackay on June 17 a very old identity in the per-son of Mr. John Jarvis Holmes. Com ing out from England on the Decca in 1887, he landed with his wife and two children, and other members of the family at Mackay. Almost immediately he went out to Allandale, plantation to work for the manager of Victoria mill, but after one month, he had the misfortune to lose his wife and elder
child. After having worked at Vic-toria mill for several months, the late Mr. Holmes went to North Eton, where he worked with his brother-in-law, the late Mr. T. Jacklin, for about 2½ years, going from there to join his father, and younger brother, Tom,  in farming operations at Woodford, where he re mained for about 40 years.  For about 20 years the late Mr. Holmes was a director of  Racecourse company, of which he was chairman for some time. He was very popular, and was well known throughout the district.     Some years after his father and mother died, the late Mr. Holmes sold the farm to Mr. T. Madigan, and came, to Mackay to reside. Being restless with city life, however, he soon re-turned to the country, and bought back the grazing part of the farm and practised cattle-raising. He lived at Mt. Vince for some years, and immedi ately, prior to his death was residing Seaforth. He leaves an only child (Mrs. E. Bridgeman, of  Toowoomba), one sister (Mrs. J. W. Harvison,  James-street), and a brother (Tom-Holmes,  Victoria-street). His eldest sister the late Mrs. T. Jacklin, died some years, ago.
