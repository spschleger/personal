18 Jul 1919 - SOLDIERS RETURN
Daily Mercury (Mackay, Qld. : 1906 - 1954), Friday 18 July 1919, page 5
SOLDIERS RETURN
A number of soldiers returned overland last night. They were Sergeant Howell, Lance Corporal Jacklin, Driver King, and Privates A. and P. Harvison. The men were met and welcomed by Mr. J. E. Joseph (chairman of the Recep-tion Committee). Privates McDon-ald and Costello are expected to ar-rive by train to-day.
