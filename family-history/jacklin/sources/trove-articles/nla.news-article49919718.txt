25 Nov 1948 - 30 students as doctors
Courier-Mail (Brisbane, Qld. : 1933 - 1954), Thursday 25 November 1948, page 5
30 students as doctors
Thirty of 42 medical students, who sat for their final examination last month, have passed and degrees will
be conferred on December 8. Of the 30, six were women. One of them, Miss Joan Earnshaw. eldest daughter of Dr. and Mrs. P. A. Earnshaw, of Clayfield, will be married t0 Dr. G. Anderson four days before she receives her degree. Dr. Anderson will go to Eng-and in January to do a post-graduate course, and Miss Earnshaw will remain in Brisbane until he returns. She will work at the Brisbane General Hospital for a year as a resident doctor. Here are the students to be ad-nitted to the Degrees of Bachelor of Medicine and Bachelor of Sur-rery at a ceremony to be held at the Medical School on December 8, at 3 p.m. — Keith Platon Archos, Douglas Forsyth Burns, Graham Bell Cavaye, Hilary Charles Fred-erick Crowley, Alan Davison, Gavin James Douglas, Joan Blandford Earnshaw, Shirley Roy Gairns, Maurice John Gallagher, Douglas James Hodges, Jessie Lurleen Hood, Marie Laura Horn, Desmond John Jacklin, Patrick Dermott Kelly, John Francis Lee, Lawrence John Lowth, Clifford Robert Lulham, Selim Abraham Mellick. Paul Wan-oistrocht Mitchell, Anne Mary O'Con-lor. Owen Watkins Powell. Henry Michael Price. Philip Bryan Robin, Samuel Roscoe Stevens. John Jo-seph Sullivan, James Nell Tennant. Laurie Trotter, James Richard Henry Watson. William Kevin Jo-seph Webb. Peter Stephen Zavat-aro.  Dr. R. K. MacPherson (38) of New Farm, will have the degree of doctor of medicine conferred upon him. He is the first graduate of the Queens-and University to be admitted to this degree. A lecturer in the Psysiology Department of the University, Dr. MacPherson received his degree as bachelor of medi-cine in 1944. Previously he had graduated as a bachelor and later master of science
