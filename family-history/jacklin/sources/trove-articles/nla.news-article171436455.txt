30 Dec 1948 - Family Notices
Daily Mercury (Mackay, Qld. : 1906 - 1954), Thursday 30 December 1948, page 2
ENGAGEMENTS BARRON— STEVENS: The engagement is announced of Doris, younger daughter of Mr. and Mrs. F. H. Stevens, Homebush-road, Mackay, to Gordon, fifth son of Mr. and Mrs. R. E. Barron, Oakey. BROWNSEY— McKENZIE: The en-gagement is announced of Elizabeth May (Betty), eldest daughter of Mr. and Mrs. W. McKenzie, 153 Nebo-road, Mackay, to Frederick George, youngest son of Mr. and Mrs. F. Brownsey, 25 Shakespeare-street, Mackay. HEEL — MENZIES : The engagement is announced of Grace Margaret, eldest daughter of Mr. and Mrs. F. G. Heel, to, Keith Thomas, elder son of Mr. and Mrs. H. Menzies. HAWKINS— JACKLIN : The engage-ment is announced of Olive, second daughter of Mr and Mrs. H. Jack-lin, Eton North, to Victor, fifth son of Mr. and Mrs. E. H. Hawkins, Eton North. JORGENSEN—REASTON: The en-gagement is announced of Patricia Mary, second youngest daughter of Mrs. L. J. Reaston, and the late Mr. J. Reaston, Walkerston, to Clive Ivan Jorgensen, second son of Mrs. P. Jorgensen and the late Mr. P. Jorgensen, North Mackay. STEVENS— JACKLIN: The engagement is announced of Irene, eldest daugh-ter of Mr. and Mrs. H. Jacklin, Eton North, to Gordon, fourth son of Mr. and Mrs. F. H. Steyens, Homebush-road, Mackay. THANKS MRS. R. BAGNALL sincerely thanks all kind friends for their ex-pressions of sympathy in her recent sad bereavement. MR. A. J. BERGMANN, Mr. and Mrs. A. Bergmann, sen., Mr, and Mrs. E. Burke, sincerely thank Doc-tors and Nursing Staff, of the Lister Hospital, for all kindnesses shown to my dear wife and our daughter dur-ing her recent illness. Also Mr. Giles for his thoughtfulness in transporting us to Springcliffe, Pastor House and Mrs. House, all relatives and friends, for all telegrams, cards, and letters of sympathy, please accept this as our personal thanks. MRS. JOE BAUD, Douglas Pitt and Family and Robert Pitt and Wife wish to thank all kind friends who sent messages of sympathy and floral tributes in their recent sad bereave-ment. Please accept this as our per-sonal thanks. DEATH BERGMANN: At Mackay on Decem-ber 26, 1945, Gwen Bergmann, dearly beloved wife of Albert Joseph Berg-mann, of Springcliffe. Deeply regretted; FUNERAL NOTICE THE relatives and friends of Mr. Robert Clark and Miss May Clark, of 11 Donaldson Street, are respectfully invited to attend the funeral of their late dear friend, Wil-liam Young Cunningham, to move from the Presbyterian Church, Mac-alister Street; this day, at 11 a.m., for Mackay Cemetery. The funeral service will commence at 10.50 a.m. Members of Mackay Sub-Branch R.S.S.A.I.L.A. are requested to at-tend.  ALEX. GRANT, Funeral Director.
