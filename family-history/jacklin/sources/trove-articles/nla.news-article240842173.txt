27 Apr 1976 - Department of Health APPOINTMENT AND CANCELLATION OF APPOINTMENT OF QUARANTINE OFFICERS
Australian Government Gazette. General (National : 1974 - 1977), Tuesday 27 April 1976 (No.G17), page 17
AIPPOUNTMENT AND CANCELLATION OF
APPOINTMENT OF QUARANTINE OFFICERS
General
HIS Excellency the Governor-General in Council has appointed Joseph Kyemenu Caiquo, Robert John Barton, Annesley Joseph Crowe, David Lawrence Ross, William Adrian Bolton, Desmond John Jacklin and Peter John Fenner as Quarantine Officers under the Quarantine Act 1908-1973
HIS Excellency the Governor-General in Council has cancelled the appointment of Annette Maria Walshe as a Quarantine Officer under the Quarantine Act 1908-1973
(Ex Min No 14)
