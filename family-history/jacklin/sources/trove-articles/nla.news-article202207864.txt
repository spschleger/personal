25 Nov 1926 - Family Notices

BIRTHS. HILL (nee Lois Murphy). —On the 17th Novem-ber, at Muswell Hill, Glen Iris, to Mr. and Mrs. Frank Hill—a son. DEATHS. CLOUGH. —On the 23rd November, at her resi-dence, Myrtle-street, Coburg, Annie, relict of late Peter Clough, loved sister of Fanny (Mrs. Cris-ford), Agnes (Mrs. Croker), Helen (Mrs. Allen). Sweet rest at last. GAYNOR. —On the 23rd November, at Euroa, John, beloved husband of Ellen Gaynor, father of John, Ellen, Patrick, Thomas, Kathleen and Mary, in his 68th year. R.I.P. GEORGE. —On the 23rd November, at 189 Bal-larat-road, Footscray, Ellen, dearly loved only child of E. and I. George, loved grandchild of Lilly and the late James Diston, loved niece of Nell, aged 5 years. Our wee Nell at rest. GEORGE. —On the 23rd November, at 189 Bal-larat-road, Footscray, Ellen Priscilla (Nellie), the loving and dearly loved only child of Ivy and Ernest George, and loving grandchild of Isabell and Albert George, aged 5 years. Privately in-terred 23rd Nov. Our faded little bud in heaven. HEALY. —On Sunday, 21st November, at her residence, Rainbow, Ann Healy, beloved mother of Frances, William, Maria (late Mrs. Taylor), Martha Matilda (Mrs. Germaine), Daniel Thomas Maginness, late of Branxbolme, aged 88 years. HEFFY. —On the 24th November, at 17 Yarra-street, North Williamstown, Margaret, the dearly beloved wife of Cornelius Heffy, and loving mother of Margaret (Mrs. W. LING. —On the 23rd November (suddenly), at his parents' residence, Wallington, Wilfred Law-rence, the dearly loved second son of Thomas and Elizabeth Ling and loving brother of Clarence, Percy and Dorothy (Mrs. Anderson, Arian Park, N.S.W.), aged 34 years. MARSDEN. —On 24th November, at Malvern, Hilton Heslop, dearly beloved husband of Annie Blanche Marsden, of "Pelaw," 5 State-street, East Malvern, and loving son-in-law of Mrs. Mary Moy-lan; also loved brother-in-law of Grace, Les, Rene, Clarrie, Mona and Kitty, aged 35 years. At rest. McALLEN. —On the 24th November, at the Children's Hospital, Melbourne, Joan, the darling little daughter of Dorian and Alice McAllen, loving sister of Gwyn; aged 2 months and 5 days. Our darling bud in heaven. McINTYRE. —On 24th November, at his resi-dence, No. 2 Westminster-street, Oakleigh, Robert McIntyre, the loved husband of Margaret Ann and loving father of Matilda, John, Joseph (deceased), James, Robert, Millie, Joseph and Isabel, late of Cobram, aged 58 years. MUNRO. —On 24th November, at his residence, 47 Cardigan-street, Carlton, Alexander, beloved hus-band of Ada Munro and loving father of Charles, Francis, James, Alexander, Hector, Stephen, aged 58 years. O'MALLEY. —On the 23rd November, at her residence, 37 Rosslyn-street, West Melbourne, Ellen, relict of the late Austin O'Malley, be-loved mother of Kate and Will, aged 81 years (a colonist of 60 years), loving grandma of Syl-via, Austin, Alma and Beryl. R.I.P. Privately interred on the 24th November. Sweet rest after years of suffering borne with Christian' fortitude. Our darling mother. O'NEILL. —On the 24th November, at the resi-dence of her son-in-law, Mr. James Brace, 65 Brunswick-street, Fitzroy, Catherine Mary, the dearly beloved wife of the late John O'Neill, of Maryborough, Victoria, formerly, of the Railway Department. Requiescat in pace. PRICE. —On the 23rd November, at Kyneton, William Henry Price of Macedon Upper, dearly loved husband of Hannah Price, and loving father of William (Macedon) and Grace (Mrs. Hayhoe, Launceston), aged 73 years. RICHARDS. —On the 24th November, at his residence, 15 Carlisle-street, East Preston, John Richards, loving husband of Janet, and dear father of Iris, Dorothy and Colin, aged 57 years. Peacefully sleeping. ROWLER. —On the 24th November, at Mel-bourne, Michael Rowler (late of Metropolitan Gas Co., South Melbourne), aged 72 years. Rest in peace. RYAN. —On the 23rd November, Frederick James Ryan, of 90 Bayswater-road, Kensington, the dearly loved husband of Louisa, and loving father of Fred, Mavis, Marie and Thelma, aged 47 years. Rest in peace. RYAN. —A tribute to our esteemed pal, Fred, who passed away on 23rd November. Sadly missed. —Inserted by his workmates. SHELDRAKE (nee Gray). —(By cable). —On the 14th November, in London, Mabel, the dearly be-loved mother of Francis, and beloved daughter of John and the late Minnie Gray, of California Gully, Bendigo, and loving sister of Edgar, Fred, Percy, Joe (deceased) Minnie and Herbie, loved sister-in-law of Kate, Annie and Eve. Sleep the sleep you so much needed, Free from sorrow, free from pain ; When we think of all you suffered, Can we wish you back again ? —Inserted by her loving brothers and sisters-in-law, Edgar and Kate, Fred and Annie Gray, Wonthaggi.
