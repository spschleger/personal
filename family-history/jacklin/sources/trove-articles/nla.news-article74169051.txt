21 Apr 1915 - Family Notices
Bairnsdale Advertiser and Tambo and Omeo Chronicle (Vic. : 1882 - 1946), Wednesday 21 April 1915, page 2
FUNERAL NOTICE. CUNNINGHAME.-The friends of the late Mr. Thomas Cunningham, are respectfully informed that his remains will be interred THIS (Wednesday) AFTERNOON.  The funeral is appointed-to leave his late residence, Swan Reach, at 1.30, for the Johnsonville Cemetery. W. SHARROW, 'Phone 27. Undertaker.
