28 Nov 1951 - Family Notices
Daily Mercury (Mackay, Qld. : 1906 - 1954), Wednesday 28 November 1951, page 2
ENGAGEMENTS JACKLIN— WARD: The engage-ment is announced of Margaret Elizabeth, younger daughter of Mr. and Mrs. C. Ward. Bairns-dale, Victoria, to Desmond John, elder son of Mr. and Mrs. A. Jacklin, Gordon Street, Mackay. FUNERAL NOTICE THE funeral of the late Henry  Pollock, of River Street, will move from the Funeral Parlour, Alfred Street, this day, at 3.30 p.m. to Glenella Park. Friends will please accept this intimation and invita-tion. Membrrs of R.S.S.A.I.L.A. are requested to attend. ALEX GRANT. Funeral Director.
