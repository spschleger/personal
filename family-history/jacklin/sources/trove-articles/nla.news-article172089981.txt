15 Feb 1952 - JACKLIN SPRINT HOPE
Daily Mercury (Mackay, Qld. : 1906 - 1954), Friday 15 February 1952, page 4
JACKLIN SPRINT HOPE
Mackay junior North Jacklin is favored to win the junior beach sprint at the Queensland titles at Townsville to-morrow.
He ran second with a lame leg in the North Barrier titles a fortnight ago. He was still lame from a knee injury he re-ceived in sprinting in the Aus-tralia Day sports. North is one of two Mackay club members who will compete in the State titles. The other is Keith Gamage, who has en-tered for the open surf ski event. North has entered for both junior and senior beach sprints and for the junior surf ski title. CERTAINTY BEATEN
Ill luck, forced him out of a chance at the State junior belt title. In the branch titles he held a lead in the junior belt event of nearly five yards when 20 yards from the buoy. He was almost totally blinded when he swam into a jelly fish and lost his strong advantage. Ambulance men had to put drops in his eyes before he could see to compete in the beach events. He later devel oped an ulcer on the cornea of his eye from the poisonous sting of the jelly fish and was three days off work. Since then he has recovered and has been in strict training for the beach sprints. He is ex-pected to meet stiff southern opposition in the senior event.
