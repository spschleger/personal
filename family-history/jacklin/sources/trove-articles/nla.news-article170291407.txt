13 Oct 1931 - Family Notices
Daily Mercury (Mackay, Qld. : 1906 - 1954), Tuesday 13 October 1931, page 4
FUNERAL NOTICE. THE funeral of the late THOMAS JACKLIN, of North Eton, will move from the Funeral Parlor, Alfred Street, This Day at 4 p.m., to the Mackay Cemetery. Friends will please accept this the only intimation and invitation. (Motor Service). ALEX GRANT, Funeral Director. IN MEMORIAM. DONNELLY.—In loving memory of Sarah Ellen Donnelly, who de-parted this life, 13th October, 1924. R.I.P. (Inserted by her loving Brothers and Sister-in-Law, Jim, Arthur, and Bella)). SCHAEFER.—In loving memory of our dear father, and grandfather, Jacob Schaefer, who passed away on 13th October, 1929. "Rest in Peace." (Inserted by the Henricks Family). DONNELLY.—In loving memory of my dear wife, Sarah Ellen, who departed this life, October 13th, 1924. R.I.P. (Inserted by her loving Husband, and Son, Eddie).
