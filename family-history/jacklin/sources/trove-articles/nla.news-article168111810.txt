07 Oct 1924 - Family Notices
Daily Mercury (Mackay, Qld. : 1906 - 1954), Tuesday 7 October 1924, page 5
MARRIAGE. TAYLOR — JACKLIN. — On Tuesday, 30th Sept., at St. Patrick Roman Catholic Church, by Rev. Dean Mul-cahy, Annie Agnes, eldest daughter of Mr. and Mrs. J. Taylor, Mackay, to Abe, second eldest son of Mr. and Mrs. Jacklin, Eton, Mackay.
IN MEMORIAM. MACKAY. — In loving memory of my dear mother, Elizabeth Mackay, who died at Gordon, Sydney, on the 7th October, 1903. "In the land o' the leal." (Inserted by her son, John Grant Mackay).
The Daily Mercury. TUESDAY, OCTOBER 7, 1924.
