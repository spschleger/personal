20 Nov 1950 - LIFESAVERS GET BRONZES
Daily Mercury (Mackay, Qld. : 1906 - 1954), Monday 20 November 1950, page 3
LIFESAVERS GET BRONZES
Seven members of the Mackny Surf Life Saving Club yesterday obtained their bronze medallions. An eighth qualified for his Instruc tor's ' certificate. They were examined by Mr. Frank Rossiter and Mr. Jack Hanlon, examiners of the North Barrier Branch, who came down
from Ayr on Friday night. The seven members who became fully fledged lifesavers by obtain ing their medallions were: — Brian William Bradshaw, jun., Norman Thomas Dew, Eric Francis Nightingale, John North Jacklin, jun., Leslie Jackson Smith, Nor man Llewellyn, Ernest Herbert McAuley, jun. Keith Ernest Lewis passed his examination for in structor. Best times for the belt and patient swims were registered by Jacklin with 2.16 and 3.10 respec tively. Mr. Rossiter was strongly impressed by his drill and swim ming ability. A strong southerly sweep near the buoys, more than 150 yards from shore, and a moderate surf, provided a strong test for the swimmers. Swimmer most affected was the last beltman, Leslie Smith. It took him 41/2 minutes to reach the buoy after being swept down some dis tance. Mr. Rossiter said after the test that the entrants had registered good times considering the condi tions under which the swim was made.
