17 Apr 1952 - PRESENTATION FROM DISTRICT FRIENDS
Bairnsdale Advertiser and East Gippsland Stock and Station Journal (Vic. : 1946 - 1954), Thursday 17 April 1952, page 3
PRESENTATION FROM
DISTRICT FRIENDS
In honor of Miss Margaret Ward, of Tambo Upper, who is to be married to Dr. Desmond Jacklan, of Macltay (Q.), on April 19, at Bairnsdale, thirty-five friends gathered at the home of Mr. and Mrs. L. G. Moon, on Tuesday, April 8. On arrival, the guest of honor was presented with a shoulder spray by Mrs.
Moon.
Several competitions conducted during the afternoon by Mrs. Mills were enjoyed, also community singing with Mrs. R. T. Redenbach as accompaniste.
On behalf of her friends in the district Mrs. Johnston conveyed to Miss Ward their very best wishes for her future happiness, and asked her to accept a silver tea service and tray for use in her future home.
Miss Ward suitably responded. Afternoon tea was served on the wide verandah from tables decorated with low bowls of flowers.
The singing of' Auld Lang Syne concluded the afternoon.
