22 Oct 1931 - OBITUARY.
Daily Mercury (Mackay, Qld. : 1906 - 1954), Thursday 22 October 1931, page 6
OBITUARY.
The death last week of Mr. Thomas Jacklin at the age- of 74 years  re-lieved from our midst a resident of the district covering a period of 48 years. Born in Lincolnshire in 1857 he arrived in Mackay with his wife, who is still hale, and hearty, on - Sep tember 13, 1883,:-by the sailing, ship. Hannah- Landells.The first, job he re-
ceived in Mackay was to assist in loading Mrs. Mitchell's, (afterwards Dame Nellie Melba). piano on to; Pugsley's wagon. His introduction to. farm life was in the employ of J. Antoney at Eton Vale. He was also employed at North Eton, Barry Estate. Eton, Victoria mill, the 'Pill- Box' owned by Mr. Dalrymple and at Far leigh. He was regarded as one of the best ploughmen in the district, and later leased land from J. Antoney on
which he grew cane, supplying North Eton Mill the first season it crushed. After a period there, he purchased freehold land at Double Peak, which he called Louth Park, after his native town in England. By a strange coincidence he later became the owner, of the land he had formerly leased from Mr. Antoney, near North Eton, and he resided, there until the time of his death. The late Mr.. Jacklin took an active part, in many branches of sport, and was a prominent rifleman. He leaves a widow who resides with her son and daughter-in-law--Mr and Mrs . H . Jacklin,(North Eton), and three sons —Harry (North Eton), George (Wal ingford.), and Abe, (Mackay). The funeral to the Mackay cemetery was well attended by relatives and. friends. The services at the grave side, were conducted by Rev. M. D. Collins.
