08 Jan 1943 - PERSONAL
Daily Mercury (Mackay, Qld. : 1906 - 1954), Friday 8 January 1943, page 2
PERSONAL
Mrs. O. McLean left Mackay yesterday for a visit to her son, Sig. P. McLean, in the south. ACW Jordan left on Monday for the South after having spent leave with her parents. Mr. and Mrs.
F. W.. Jordan, Yakapari. Mr. and Mrs. A. E. Crofts, of West Bundaberg, are visiting Mackay as guests of Mrs. Crofts's mother, Mr. J. Christie, "Rothie-may."  Mr. and Mrs. P. Davis, of Marryatt-street, have been advised that their son Private P. D. Davis who has been on duty over seas, has returned to Australia. Mackay lads who have achieved outstanding success in academic and sporting training at southern colleges include Nudgee student Desmond Jacklin. son of Mr. and Mrs. Abe Jacklin, Gordon-street. Young Jacklin secured a high pass in last year's senior exam and his sporting activities at the college include victory in the 100 yards memorial handicap, first in the 120yds. championship hurdles, first in the 50 metres back stroke swimming championship, second in the high jump title, sec-ond in the 120yds. open all-school hurdles, third in the all-round championship cup, and a succes-sion of places in a variety of other all-round sporting events. He won the first honor blazer for football and athletics.
Mrs. J. Mather, of Pindi Pindi, has been offi-cially notified that her eldest son, Corporal Robt. Mather, AIF, was killed in action in New Guinea on November 9.
