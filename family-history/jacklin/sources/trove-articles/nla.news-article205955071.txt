09 Nov 1936 - Mrs. MARGARET CUNNINGHAM.
Age (Melbourne, Vic. : 1854 - 1954), Monday 9 November 1936, page 12
Mrs. MARGARET CUNNINGHAM.
At the age of 97, the death occurred at the residence of her daughter, Mrs. W. Byrne, Swan Reach, of Mrs. Margaret Cunningham, wife of the late Mr. Thomas Cunningham, who predeceased her by 21 years. Born at Kiandra, New South Wales, Mrs. Cunningham with her
husband crossed the border in 1867 and settled on the Tambo River. They followed farming pursuits and reared a family of six sons and four daughters. The burial took place at Bumberrah Cemetery.
