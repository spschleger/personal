RECOVERY OF CAR FARE.
Thomas Kavanagh appeared in: the Police Court yesterday morning,- and (pleaded . guilty to1 a charge of haying, refused to pay £2, due to Abe Jacklin,
.motor car proprietor. In pleading defendant added 'that he had intended paying the money on the previous night, but he was ' locked up too soon. — Sub-Inspector Farrell said action was taken :under the Traffic Act. On the. previous afternoon defendant engaged Jacklin, who drove him about, ..the 'time occupied in doing so being, three hours,' for which Jacklin charged -him £2. When: his engagement terminated the car-driver demanded his fare, but defendant refused to pay.- Jacklin: had jthen no other alternative but to call the assistance of the police, and defendant)'-after search, was found at the Show Grounds. He was reasoned with, arid1 given every opportunity to pay, which' he' said he refused to -do. — Defendant was ordered to pay the, amount: due, arid also 5/- witness's expenses.
