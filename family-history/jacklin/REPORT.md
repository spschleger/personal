## 1. Desmond John Jacklin (1926 – 9 Sep 1981) & Margaret Elizabeth Ward (c.1927 – 2025)
- Desmond b. 1926 (Qld death index dob "1926"), elder son of Abraham & Annie Agnes, Gordon St Mackay. Page boy at uncle's Taylor–Owen wedding Dec 1927 [A]. State scholarship 1939 (Mackay Centre) [A]. Nudgee College Brisbane — high senior pass 1942, 100 yd sprint win [B]. **MB BS Univ. of Queensland, graduated Nov/Dec 1948** (Courier-Mail 25 Nov 1948) [A]. QSA holds "JACKLIN D.J. (Medical)" registration file 1948–50 (ITM1659079).
- Senior resident medical officer / acting medical superintendent, Mackay District Hospital 1953; resigned Nov 1953; **private practice at 73 Evans Ave, North Mackay from 25 Jan 1954**; residence Bridge Rd 1952 [A]. Keen angler ("Angling Antics").
- Engagement announced 28 Nov 1951 (Daily Mercury) / 14 Dec 1951 (Sun News-Pictorial): Margaret Elizabeth, younger dau. of Mr & Mrs C. W. Ward, Tambo Upper (Bairnsdale) [A]. **Married 19 Apr 1952 at Bairnsdale** (Bairnsdale Advertiser 17 Apr 1952 presentation; Vic marriage index 8334/1952) [A]. Two children by Oct 1955 (Telegraph 15 Oct 1955 — Mrs Des Jacklin matron of honour, Hobart wedding) [A].
- Desmond d. 9 Sep 1981 Qld (reg 56006/1981; QSA estate file "JACKLIN, DESMOND JOHN" 1981, ITM946943; Newcrest shareholder listing 1994 c/- Macrossan & Amiet, Mackay) [A].
- Margaret: Ryerson Index — **Margaret Elizabeth Jacklin, probate notice 3 Oct 2025, late of Beaconsfield, Qld** (Mackay suburb) [A]. Childhood: Tambo Upper Sunday School concert 1933; "Pet Letter to Hamish" (Sun News-Pictorial 16 Nov 1935 — a letter written as the family pig, signed Margaret Ward, Tambo Upper); gymkhana ladies' race 1944; bridesmaid at sister Mary's wedding Dec 1944 [A/B].
- Uncle: North Jacklin — Nudgee College 1947–49 (GPS high-jump/swimming record-holder), Mackay SLSC junior beach-sprint champion 1950–52, club captain 1953–54, worked at Fields' Motors from c.1951, farewelled Sept 1952 (Townsville posting) by Dr & Mrs D. Jacklin, Bridge Rd; **founded North Jacklin (B.M.C. franchise) April 1961 with wife Judy** — 60th anniversary 20 Apr 2021 [A/B].

## 2. Abraham "Abe" Jacklin (9 Oct 1891 – 18 Mar 1962) & Ann Agnes Taylor (19 Dec 1894 – 19 Jul 1971)
- Abraham b. 9 Oct 1891 (C8220/1891) to Thomas Jacklin & Fanny Holmes; an earlier Abraham b. 13 Aug 1890 d. 20 Jun 1891 (C2681/1891) [A]. Second-eldest surviving son; brothers Thomas George (1886–1965, "George", m. Sophie Hansen 1908, farmer Wallingford/Pleystowe → the West Mackay Jacklins incl. George Douglas "Doug" 1918–2007) and Henry "Harry" (1898–1986, North Eton, m. Ann Richardson Clark 1926) [A].
- **WWI AIF: "Cpl. Abe Jacklin, Mackay" — casualty lists Dec 1917 (wounded) and 27 Jun 1918 (gassed, 2nd occasion)** [A]. Service record = NAA B2455 (not yet pulled).
- Motor-car proprietor Mackay 1924 [A]. **m. 30 Sep 1924 St Patrick's RC Mackay** (C3285/1924; best man brother Harry; bridesmaid "Tot" Taylor; Dr Taylor, med. supt Townsville Hospital, brother of bride) [A].
- Licensee: Albion Hotel Walkerston (to Nov 1926), Cremorne Hotel North Side (1927–29), 34/14 Gordon St Mackay (1933–34), Carmila (1935); "Jacklin's Flats, Gordon Street"; theatre exhibitor 1946 (Four Ways Theatre, Nebo Rd, hosted by J. Taylor & N. Jacklin 1950). Executor of father's will 1931 (£3249) [A]. d. 18 Mar 1962 (C823/1962) [A].
- Ann Agnes: eldest dau. of John Taylor & Mary North, Queensland Hotel, Mackay. Lived at the hotel with mother 1931 (theatre-takings robbery case) [A]. Ladies' Suburban Bowling Club. d. 19 Jul 1971 (C4873/1971); QSA file "JACKLIN, ANNIE AGNES" 1972 [A].
- Children: Desmond John (1926) and John North (c.1932). Only two identified.

## 3. Thomas Jacklin (1857 – 13 Oct 1931) & Fanny Margaret Holmes (c.1858 – 24 Jan 1933)
- Obituary (Daily Mercury 22 Oct 1931): **born Lincolnshire 1857; native town Louth; arrived Mackay 13 Sep 1883 with his wife by the sailing ship *Hannah Landells***; first job loading Mrs Mitchell's (Nellie Melba's) piano; farm hand Eton Vale (J. Antoney), North Eton, Barry Estate, Victoria Mill, "Pill Box", Farleigh; "one of the best ploughmen in the district"; leased then bought land at North Eton; freehold at Double Peak named **Louth Park**; supplied North Eton Mill first season; prominent rifleman. Widow living with son Harry [B].
- Death reg C3913/1931: parents **Abraham Jacklin & Elizabeth Skipworth** [A]. Probate to sons T. G., Abraham & Henry, £3249 [A].
- Brother **William Jacklin** (m. Elizabeth Hall 1886; d. 30 Nov 1942, parents Abraham & Maria Skipworth) → the Ipswich/Gatton/Glenore Grove Jacklins (Roy Thomas 1923–2014, Stella, Val, Francis Cecil etc.). Also James Jacklin "formerly of Gayton-le-Marsh, Lincoln" m. Emma Marston Ipswich 1889 — same Lincolnshire cluster, relationship unproven [A/C].
- Fanny Margaret: death C577/1933, father **Langley Holmes** [A]. Eldest sister of John Jarvis Holmes (obit 26 Jun 1937: Holmes family came out on the *Decca* 1887 to Mackay; JJH worked 2½ yrs with brother-in-law T. Jacklin at North Eton; farm at Woodford) [B]. Mother "Mrs Holmes senr", d. 31 Jan 1918 aged ~79 at dau. Mrs Harvison's, Woodford = **Fanny Holmes née Shepherd, dau. of Thomas Grant Shepherd** (C658/1918); father Langley Holmes (b. c.1844, "in his 74th year" 1918) d. 27 Apr 1924 (C1779/1924) [A].
- Children (Qld index): Thomas George 17 Jun 1886; Abraham 1890 (d. inf.); Abraham 9 Oct 1891; Henry 21 Oct 1898. Others may have been born England pre-1883 (none found).

## Technical notes (for reuse)
Qld BDM: POST https://www.familyhistory.bdm.qld.gov.au/search with X-CSRF-TOKEN from meta `_csrf`, JSON `{count:20,page,subjectfamilyname,recordtype:births|marriages|deaths,doefrom,doeto,...}` — **use doefrom/doeto (event date), not dobfrom/dobto** (dob filter silently drops records with no DOB — deaths/marriages). Script: sources/qbdm.py.
Trove: web API `GET https://trove.nla.gov.au/api/search/137?terms=..&pageSize=100&startPos=..&limits={"state":["Queensland"]}` header `apikey = md5("Wonder"+x-ctx cookie).lstrip("0")`; article text at `/newspaper/rendition/nla.news-article{id}.txt`. Script: sources/trove_harvest.py.
Vic BDM: SPA at my.rio.bdm.vic.gov.au; API `POST https://api.rio.bdm.vic.gov.au/service/epublic/efam/efamily/search/{uuid}` with cookie credentials (drive from a page-context fetch in Chrome); body `{person:{name:{familyName,firstGivenName}},father/mother/spouse:{name:{familyNameAtBirth|firstGivenName|familyName}},isBirth,isDeath,isMarriage,yearFrom,yearTo,place,type:"Historical events",username,requestId}`; max 100 results; births indexed only to ~1926.
Ryerson: POST https://ryersonindex.org/search.php (search_sn=…, all fields present) after GET for cookie; 429 if hammered.
QSA ArchivesSearch: browse `/search?f[]=keywords&q[]=Jacklin` (page param 0-based); API is captcha-gated.
Trove page images (added 18 Aug, later): full-res scans are tiled. Metadata: `GET https://trove.nla.gov.au/newspaper/image/info/{pageId}` (XML: levels 0–7, colmin/colmax/rowmin/rowmax, xoffset/yoffset, tilesize 256; level 7 = full res). Tiles: `https://trove.nla.gov.au/imageservice/nla.news-page{pageId}/tile{level}-{col}-{row}` (page origin = xoffset−colmin·256, yoffset−rowmin·256). pageId is in the article page HTML (`nla.news-page…`). `/ndp/imageservice/nla.news-page{id}/print` gives a low-res PDF only. Script: sources/trove_page_tiles.py (used for the 1935 Sun pig letter, saved as sources/1935-11-16_sun-news-pictorial_pet-letter-to-hamish_margaret-ward.jpg).
